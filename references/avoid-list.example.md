# Avoid-list — EXAMPLE ONLY

> **This is one person's list, reproduced as an example of the format. It is not advice, not
> a default, and not what the skill loads.** The skill reads `avoid-list.md`, which ships
> empty. Some entries below have a solid regulatory basis and some are habit; the author
> keeps them anyway and would not ask anyone else to. Write your own, and write it before
> you scan anything.

Standing list of ingredients/additives to flag by default when found on a label. Treat it as living, not fixed.

## Food & beverage

- MSG (monosodium glutamate)
- Sulfites / sulfur dioxide / sodium sulfite
- Nitrates / nitrites
- Artificial dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1, etc.) and titanium dioxide
- Seed/vegetable oils: soybean oil, canola oil, palm oil
- Artificial sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose, etc.) and carrageenan

## Cosmetics & personal care

- Parabens and phthalates
- Synthetic fragrance ("parfum"/"fragrance") and PFAS
- Aluminum compounds (e.g. in antiperspirants) and talc

## Household — not specified

No standing flags here. When a household product comes up for the first time, ask what to watch for (common candidates: PFAS, phthalates, VOCs, quaternary ammonium compounds, chlorine bleach, flame retardants) and record the answer.

## Notes

- Distinguish, when flagging: regulatory-restricted (banned/restricted somewhere, e.g. EU) vs. commonly-avoided-but-unrestricted (e.g. MSG). Don't present the two the same way.
- This list reflects personal preference, not a claim that every item here is dangerous at typical exposure levels — say so if asked.
